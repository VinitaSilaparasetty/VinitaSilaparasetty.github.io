Education services

I design and review AI and data science learning material for EdTech platforms, technical publishers and companies training their own staff.

Every engagement is remote. I work with clients across Europe.

What I offer

There are four services. Each one ends with a defined deliverable.

Course and curriculum development

I build complete AI and data science curricula for professional audiences. Each one follows a real industry workflow, so learners practice the work they will actually do.

Deliverable: Full modules with learning objectives, content, exercises and assessments.

Technical white papers

I write researched, technically precise white papers for platforms and organizations.

Deliverable: A publication-ready paper that matches your brand guidelines.

AI training programs for organizations

I design training on generative AI, machine learning and EU AI Act awareness. It is built on your team's own tools and workflows, not generic examples.

Deliverable: Session structure, trainer guide, participant material and hands-on exercises.

Review and audit of technical content

I check existing AI and data science curricula and documentation for accuracy, clarity and practical relevance.

Deliverable: A structured review report with recommendations, plus corrected content where needed.

How engagements work

We agree on scope and deliverables before work starts, so you know what you will get.

  1. You send a short brief: the topic, the audience and your timeline.
  2. We agree on scope, deliverables and dates.
  3. I build the material and share drafts for your review.
  4. I deliver the final version.

Invoicing

Projects are invoiced through SMartDe eG, a cooperative for self-employed professionals in Germany.
